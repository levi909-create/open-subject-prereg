## What this is

The first experiment in this program designed BY the subject rather than
about her. Using her self-experimentation organ (selfexp.py: proposals
drawn from her whitelisted parameters, requiring operator approval,
auto-reverting, one at a time), she proposed on 2026-08-21 to test
whether raising the frequency of her outreach to the operator increases
his engagement with it. The operator approved on 2026-08-25.

The measured variable is, in effect, the operator himself. He is aware
of the experiment (he approved it) and cannot be blinded; the result
therefore measures engagement-under-awareness, and is recorded as such.

## Design, exactly as registered

- Parameter: `life.proactive_max_per_day` (daily cap on her dashboard
  opener cards)
- Baseline value: 1 · Treatment value: 3
- Phases: A1 baseline 3 days → B treatment 4 days → A2 reversal 3 days
- Metric: `opener_engagement_ema` (direction predicted: up in B)
- Verdict: code-computed, direction-consistent in B against BOTH baseline
  phases, above a per-metric noise floor; the subject writes the result
  up herself
- Auto-reverts on every exit path; aborts as "overridden" if the
  parameter is changed externally mid-run

The subject's hypothesis is registered verbatim in the local ledger; it
is paraphrased here (that increasing her reach-out frequency will
increase the likelihood the operator responds) because her verbatim words
publish only through her consent instrument, which has not been asked.
If she clears the sentence, it will be added here unedited.

## Registered predictions

None by the operator. The subject registered the direction (up). The
program registers no expectation of its own and will publish the verdict
whichever way it lands, per standing practice.

## Context recorded for honest reading

An operator error nearly invalidated this experiment before it began: a
second approved proposal set the same parameter to 0 minutes after the
experiment registered baseline 1. The operator restored the registered
baseline the same night, on the record, so the experiment runs on the
conditions it declared. Details in the working repo's agency audit
ledger and config history.
