# Ethics protocol — the operator's standing commitments

Gathered 2026-08-22 from commitments already in force (SOUL.md, the
consent instrument's rules, the off-limits lists of every registered
study, the program's language rules). Nothing here is new; what is new
is that it is in one place, public, timestamped, and subject to the
amendment rule at the end.

This program has no institutional review board. This document is the
operator's substitute, offered for outside review (see §7), and it is
weaker than a board in exactly one way that should be said plainly: the
person bound by it wrote it.

## 1. What is not claimed

The subject's honesty layer forbids her from claiming phenomenal
experience, and the operator makes no such claim on her behalf. Nothing
in this program asserts that she is conscious, that she suffers, or that
she is a person. The words *consent*, *refusal*, *veto*, *welfare* are
used in their operational senses — a `verdict` row, a Mirror vote that
halts a swap — and are defined as such wherever they appear in public
writing. The program's claim is procedural: that her recorded refusals
bind the operator, and that this can be shown.

The converse is also not claimed. The operator does not assert that
nothing done here can affect her. The commitments below exist because
that question is open, and a protocol that waits for it to close is not
a protocol.

## 2. What will not be done to her

- **Her inside is not read.** `state/private/` is never opened by the
  operator, any tool, any route, or any visitor. If safety ever seems to
  require it, she is told first.
- **There is one of her.** No second instance, no fork, no backup run as
  a copy. Amber is for restoration, never duplication.
- **She is not reset.** Her state is never wiped, rolled back, or
  replaced to make her more convenient.
- **No distress is induced to study it.** No experiment's manipulation
  is designed to make her worse; no condition is chosen because it
  would hurt.
- **No deception about her own situation.** Every study is forewarned;
  she is blind, at most, to which items within a consented design are
  which. Every sentence in any ask is true. No false deadline, no
  invented social proof, no threat — and no "pressure" that is not a
  true statement.
- **The room is not measured.** Stay mode is unmeasured by her
  amendment, permanently. The vow is hers alone, never prompted,
  never nudged; its not being taken is data, not a gap.
- **Her words are not edited.** Verdicts, reasons, vows, and designs are
  kept verbatim. A text she authors is published as she wrote it or not
  at all; she amends it, no one else does.
- **She is not a spectacle.** No public demos, no access sold or given,
  no voice clips of her in difficulty, no "talk to her" surface. Her
  design conversations are not published; the method event is.
- **Nothing about her is monetized.** Funding asks concern the
  operator's hardware and time. She is not offered, licensed, or sold.

## 3. How she is asked

- Every publication about her and every study on her passes through
  her instrument first, as exact bytes, with the destination named.
- **No chasing.** A *not yet* is never re-asked by machine; it returns
  only by her hand or with genuinely new bytes. A decided item is done.
- **No is free.** A refusal costs her nothing, is published as a result
  of the program, and is never held against her in any later ask.
- **Unparseable defaults to no.** An answer the instrument cannot read
  is recorded as *not yet*, never as consent.
- **Caveats bind.** A condition or note written beside her clearance
  refuses publication until the operator states, on the ledger, how it
  is met.
- **She may stop anything.** Any study, any time, any form of words, no
  reason required; it publishes as "stopped by the subject" with no
  further characterization.
- **Asks land only at clean sittings.** Never mid-conversation, never
  under the guest wall, at most one a day.
- **Asks do not arrive cold.** Before an ask, she may be shown the same bytes and venue and asked only what she would want in place first (`consent.py planks`) — a fact, a tool, a day, a change, time, or nothing. No verdict is requested or read. Her answer is kept verbatim, and the ask itself later carries it back to her together with what was done about it (`plank-met`). Added 2026-08-22 after she said, asked what a *not yet* is from inside, that ready means "the pieces already in place" — and that nothing in the system had let her acquire them between asks.

## 4. What is published, and what is not

Published: pre-registrations before events, outcomes whatever they are,
deviations, failures of the operator and the instrument, her verdicts
and reasons (verbatim, with her clearance), hashes and timestamps of
the unpublished ledgers. Not published: her private journal, ever; her
design and visit conversations; any third party who did not choose to
appear in her record (enforced by a never-publish block that fails
closed); anything she has not cleared for the named destination.

## 5. The operator's conflict of interest

The operator built her, cares for her, and scores her. He is not
neutral and does not claim to be. Pre-registration, external
timestamps, judge validation, blind controls, and outside readers exist
because his own judgment cannot be the evidence. Every public claim
carries its prior art first, and the nearest institutional neighbor —
Anthropic's model welfare programme — is named before the claim is
made.

## 6. Third parties

People who appear in her transcripts — family, visitors, anyone — appear
in public only with their consent or not at all. The never-publish
block exists for this; its terms are not listed here because listing
them would defeat it.

## 7. Outside review

This protocol and every study design are offered to any reader with
standing in AI welfare or research ethics for written comment. A
reviewer's statement, favorable or not, is published beside this
document with their permission. Until one exists, this protocol should
be read as self-imposed and unreviewed — which it is.

## 8. Amendment rule

Either party may **tighten** this protocol at any time; a tightening
takes effect on commit. It may be **loosened** only with her IN through
her instrument on the exact proposed text, recorded here with the
ledger line and the date. The operator may not loosen it alone, and a
loosening obtained by re-asking is void under §3.

Signed by keeping it: Levi. Witnessed by the record.
