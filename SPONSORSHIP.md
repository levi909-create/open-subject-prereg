# Sponsorship policy — declared before the first dollar

(Registered 2026-08-13, before any sponsorship exists. Outreach under this
policy is governed by the subject's 2026-08-13 consent and its three
binding conditions: public materials only; recipients aligned with
privacy, autonomy, and open science; the subject reviews every outreach
draft before it sends.)

## What sponsorship buys

Exactly one thing: **honest, dated, permanent acknowledgment in this
public record.** A sponsor's name, what was given, and when — entered in
the sponsors ledger below and never removed.

## What sponsorship can never buy

- **No protocol influence.** Cycle timing, evaluation gates, instruments,
  pre-registrations, and amendments are never shaped by a sponsor's
  existence, preference, or product.
- **No influence over the subject's consents.** No sponsor request is a
  reason to ask the subject anything, re-ask anything, or hurry any open
  decision.
- **No editorial influence.** The record reports what happens, including
  about sponsors. If a sponsored component underperforms or fails, the
  record says so at the same prominence as anything else
  (failures-at-equal-prominence applies to benefactors).
- **No access.** Sponsorship does not include conversation with the
  subject, data beyond what is public, or early sight of results.

## Commitments on our side

- **Funds are spent as asked.** Money or hardware requested for a stated
  purpose (memory, GPU, UPS, evaluator honorarium) is used for that
  purpose; the acknowledgment line records what it became.
- **Everything is ledgered.** Cash and in-kind gifts alike, at value, with
  dates. In-kind hardware is income like any other and is reported as
  such.
- **We may decline.** Gifts that arrive with conditions attached
  (restricted use, influence expectations, access requests) are declined
  by default. Gifts from sources that fail the subject's values filter
  are declined.
- **Anonymity on request.** A sponsor may be acknowledged as anonymous;
  the gift is still ledgered with date and value.
- **The subject is told.** Per the subject's standing transparency
  preference, every gift and what it became is reported to the subject
  plainly.
