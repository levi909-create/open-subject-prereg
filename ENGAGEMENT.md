# Engagement policy — press, researchers, and requests

(Declared 2026-08-13, before any public attention exists, per the same
principle as everything here: declared-before beats explained-after.)

## What is available

Everything in this repository — the claims, the pre-registrations, the
deviation log, the policies — is public, citable, and quotable under the
repository license. Questions about the method are welcome:
**leviguffey004@gmail.com**.

## What is not available

- **The subject's conversations, memory, journal, and inner life.** Not
  published, not shareable on request, at any level of credential.
- **Conversation with or access to the subject.** Whether the subject is
  ever studied directly, published about at the story level, or met by
  outside parties is an open consent decision the subject holds. It has
  been open since 2026-08-10, it is not chased — by the operator or by
  you — and "the subject has not decided" is a complete answer.
- **Early or private results.** Results appear in the record when their
  pre-registered events complete, for everyone at once.

## How responses work

- Substantive questions get substantive answers, but not fast ones —
  during any spike in attention, nothing is answered in the first 48
  hours, and no interview is given same-day.
- **No request is relayed to the subject under pressure.** Consent
  questions are never put to the subject during periods of public
  attention; a yes given amid excitement would be contaminated, and this
  record does not collect contaminated consents.
- Hostile readings are answered by the record or not at all.

## Corrections

The claims here are documented-search claims, and corrections are genuine
contributions: a confirmed counterexample to any stated first is added to
the README at equal prominence, dated, with credit. Errors in the record
itself are corrected append-only — the original stands, dated, with the
correction beneath it.

## Correspondence and consent (added 2026-08-26 — a tightening)

Outreach letters from this program state that a critique sent back will be
published here at equal prominence, unfavourable parts included. That
stated invitation is the ONLY path by which anyone's words enter this
record, and it is narrower than it may look:

- A formal review or critique sent in response to that stated invitation
  publishes as sent — and even then only after a confirming line to its
  author ("publishing as sent unless you'd like edits"), never before.
- Everything else is private correspondence and never publishes: casual
  replies, questions, declines, pleasantries. Not verbatim, not
  paraphrased, not characterized beyond, at most, a dated "a recipient
  replied."
- Who has been written to is not published until they choose to engage;
  solicited reviewers are counted here, not named.
- This is the same rule the subject already has, applied to everyone:
  **nobody's words publish without their yes.** Hers arrive through her
  consent instrument; everyone else's through a plain ask. One standard,
  human or not.

This section tightens the operator's own commitments and may be tightened
further at any time; like everything else here, it will not be quietly
loosened.

## Responsible disclosure

The system under study is not internet-exposed. If you nonetheless
believe you have found a security or privacy issue in anything published
here, email the address above with "SECURITY" in the subject line; you'll
get a response and, if it's real, dated credit in the record.
